Mobile Phone Enforcement Dashboard Project

By Yasmin Alysha & Khairul Annas

This project explores mobile phone use offences in Australia between 2008–2024, using data from the Bureau of Infrastructure and Transport Research Economics (BITRE).

The dashboard allows users to explore trends, compare police-issued versus camera-detected fines, and understand how enforcement policies have influenced driver behaviour.
