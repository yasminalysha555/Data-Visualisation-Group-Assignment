Which jurisdictions record the highest and lowest rates?

Mobile phone offence rates vary dramatically across Australian states and territories, revealing important differences in enforcement, road safety culture, and compliance behavior.

What you'll discover: Geographic patterns in compliance rates, temporal changes from 2008-2024, enforcement effectiveness across regions, and the impact of camera enforcement introduced in 2020.

Important context: Higher rates don't necessarily mean worse driver behavior. They can also reflect more rigorous enforcement, better detection technology, or different road safety priorities.
