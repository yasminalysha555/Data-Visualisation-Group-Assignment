About Page

This project is a direct response to the Bureau of Infrastructure and Transport Research Economics' (BITRE) expanded data collection efforts. While BITRE has historically published road safety enforcement statistics, the 2023 update introduced a new level of detail—including location, age group, arrests, and charges.


Our objective is to design and implement interactive visualizations that do two key things:

1. Allow Exploration: Give users the ability to deeply explore selected parts of this new, fine-grained enforcement data.

2. Tell a Story: Transform complex data points into clear, visual stories about current road safety enforcement trends.


This project focuses on delivering a highly usable and effective new website that makes the valuable 2023 data accessible and understandable to everyone.
