About Page

This project analyses mobile phone non-compliance offences in Australia using enforcement data published by the Bureau of Infrastructure and Transport Research Economics (BITRE). The dataset spans 2008 to 2024 and captures the number of fines issued by police and camera-based detection methods across all states and territories. Mobile phone use while driving remains a major road safety concern, and long-term data is essential to understanding how enforcement practices and driver behaviour have evolved.


Our objective is to design and implement interactive visualizations that do two key things:

1. Allow Exploration: Give users the ability to explore mobile phone offence patterns across jurisdictions and over time using BITRE’s national enforcement dataset.

2. Tell a Story: Transform complex data points into clear, visual stories about current road safety enforcement trends.


This project focuses on delivering a usable, intuitive, and visually clear website that turns raw enforcement data into insights, helping users better understand the state of mobile-phone-related road safety in Australia.
